Ella Barker (b.&nbsp;2001) is an emerging painter practising in Naarm&nbsp;/&nbsp;Melbourne. She works primarily with oils on canvas, painting figures held in ordinary, in-between moments.

She recently graduated from the Royal Melbourne Institute of Technology with a Bachelor of Fine Arts (Painting) in 2024, showing at the annual RMIT graduation exhibition — where she received the Chapman&nbsp;&amp;&nbsp;Bailey Award.

She was also included in the *Homage to Turner* group show (2025) at West End Art Space. Ella's paintings are held in various private collections.
